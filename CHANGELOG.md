# Music Video Capture Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Capture a YouTube music video as a tagged `.m4a` with square cover art, in the background.
- Save to a capture folder, an artist folder, artist/album, or any folder; each channel remembers its
  last destination.
- Suggest the album: YouTube's own, "{year} Mixes" for long mixes, or none; tag every capture's Grouping.
- Remount a network share (SMB or AFP) when needed, and optionally add the song to Apple Music.
